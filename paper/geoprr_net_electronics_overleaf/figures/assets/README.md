# Figure example provenance

`rf100_test_000000.png` is a cropped gauge ROI from the public
`needle-base-tip-min-max` test split distributed through RF100-VL. It is used
only as an illustrative input in Figure 1. The blur and projective variants in
that figure are generated by `../build_geoprr_figures.py`; they are not
additional observations or quantitative results.

RF100-VL is cited in the manuscript as Popov et al. (NeurIPS 2025). The
RF100-VL repository distributes its constituent datasets under Apache-2.0;
the local Roboflow export also includes its own dataset license metadata.
